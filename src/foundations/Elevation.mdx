# Elevation: shadow and gradients

The system uses **one shadow** and **four gradients**. All are built from semantic colours with `color-mix`, so they follow the theme.

## Rules

1. Depth comes from colour and surface, not from shadow. Do not add shadows to cards or buttons.
2. Text over a photo always sits on a scrim. Never place white text on an unprotected photo.
3. Gradients and the shadow cannot be bound to variables in Figma. They exist as Figma styles (`Gradient/*`, `Shadow/nav-scrolled`) and as these CSS tokens: keep both in step.
